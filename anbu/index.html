Our Story

Three generations, one wood-fired stove

Anbu Chettinad Kitchen began in 1998 as a six-table eatery run by Paati Anbu herself. Her hand-pounded masalas and refusal to rush a curry built a following that outgrew the little shop in three years.

Today her grandchildren run the kitchen — same recipes, same fire, just more tables. Nothing here comes from a packet. Ever.
